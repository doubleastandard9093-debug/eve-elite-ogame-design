# 05 — Research Tree
There are four branches. Each node costs resources and time, and only one research job runs per player at a time.
- **Propulsion:** Ion Drive → Slipstream Drive → Lattice Drive (capital jumps)
- **Industry:** Refining, Fabrication Efficiency, Extra Queues, Astro-Logistics (more colony slots)
- **Combat:** weapon tiers (kinetic, thermal, plasma), shields, armor, Electronic Warfare
- **Science:** Scanning, Exotics handling, Espionage, Research Network (link several labs together)

Research applies to the whole account. Ship piloting skills are kept separate (see doc 07).
