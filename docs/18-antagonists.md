# 18 — Antagonists (§7E)
Source: Aaron's design update, 2026-10-27.

Four populations, not a spawn table:
| Population | Behaviour | Trade channel |
|---|---|---|
| **Rogues** | Opportunists who flee. | Rogue fences. |
| **Pirates** | Organized, with a chain of command and their own logistics. Pirate bases can be taken over. | Pirate markets with **flagged goods**: cheap stolen cargo that carries a legal mark in lawful space. |
| **Ancient sentinels** | Guard; never pursue, never retreat. | Researcher bounties for sentinel cores. |
| **Shadow factions** | No territory, no signature until they choose to reveal. What they want is rarely cargo. | Shadow contracts paid in standing and information, so a covert-ops pilot has an economy without ever being seen. |

- **Spawn pressure is economic, not random.** Rogue and pirate activity scales with regional wealth and traffic. The encounter rate you feel is a readout of the economy, and claim owners get a reason to fund patrols, not just more guns.
- **Every faction opens a trade channel**, not just a fight.
