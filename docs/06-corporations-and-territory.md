# 06 — Syndicates & Territory
- **Syndicates:** groups with ranks, a shared hangar, a treasury, taxes, and diplomacy (allied, neutral, or hostile). Several Syndicates can join into a **Coalition**.
- **Security bands:** **Secured** (Sec Space), **Restricted** (Rest Space), **Borderland** (Bor Space) and **Frontier** (The Black) (2026-10 update). PVP rules per band are in doc 15. Secured has free NPC-owned lanes and police; Frontier has no protection and the richest claims.
- **Claiming:** you anchor a **Beacon** in a Borderland or Frontier system. Upgrades add mining, industry, and defense bonuses.
- **Sieges:** attacking a Beacon opens a vulnerability window set in advance. The structure goes to reinforced mode for 24–48 hours, then a final battle at a fixed time decides it. This lets both sides plan.
- **Logistics:** territory gives Syndicates jump bridges and staging stations, and they pay upkeep in resources.

- **Claim defense:** a claim is defended by a Defense Budget from output and residency, not a garrison (doc 15 §7).
- **Frontier claims beyond the gate network:** small ships can only get there through wormholes, and capitals by self-jump. Holding a Frontier claim means keeping wormhole routes charted and defended, plus fuel for capital jumps (docs 13, 19).
- **Lanes:** players build and toll lanes where the NPC network doesn't reach (doc 13).
