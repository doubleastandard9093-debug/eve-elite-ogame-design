# 14 — Ships: Supply, Visible Modules & Interiors
Source: Aaron's design update, 2026-10-27.

## Ship range
- Many kinds, from fighters up to capital behemoths.
- A dedicated **mining line**, from small miners to massive haulers.
- Players can build most ships. Some ships can only be bought from the faction they belong to.
- Ships are mostly **predesigned**, but modules change appearance and add or remove hardpoints. Hardpoints and modules change **ship stability**, which you balance in your buildout.

## Appearance is a build readout
Fitted modules drive the visible configuration with no editor:
- Gun hardpoints render the actual weapon models.
- Support bays change hull geometry.
- Utility modules show as masts and pods.
- An ore hold or salvage bay replaces the rear section.
- Engine nozzles change with the drive.

So a Miner and a Hauler on the same cruiser base look clearly different, and a foreman ship with its mining wing looks like a formation rather than five identical cruisers.

**Silhouette is intel.** Reading a hull's fit from outside weapons range is a real skill in claimed-volume fights (doc 15). A fitted interdictor is visible from outside, which is exactly what a lane pilot needs to know.

## Supply split: three tiers
| Tier | Source | Contents | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Baseline hulls** (~40% of catalogue) | NPC factions | Every corvette and frigate; basic cruiser line (Miner, Hauler, Extractor, Surveyor); one cheap line battlecruiser per faction | Sold bare, zero modules. Guarantees a flyable hull on day one, so a loss is never terminal (pillar 4). |
| **Faction-specific hulls** | Owning faction only, standing-gated | One or two signature hulls per faction, distinct silhouette, one strong quirk, never best-in-class overall | Can be gifted, lost, salvaged and rebuilt, but **not sold** on the open market. Salvage is the only way one changes hands. |
| **Advanced & specialist hulls** | Players only | Destroyers and up, covert ops, industrial command, battleships, all capitals | Every capital traces back to player mining, refining and a player-built shipyard. |

Three rules:
1. **NPC hulls are bare frames.** You can buy a hull from a faction, never a fighting ship.
2. **NPC prices are a soft floor**, not a competitor. No NPC undercuts player industry on a hull players can build.
3. **Salvage is the third source** of hulls (doc 16), for early-game supply and post-war rearmament.

## Modifications and repair
- Ship modifications happen at a station or base that has the facilities.
- Skilled players, or drones, can do some repairs in space.

## Ship interiors
- Third-person, first-person or VR view.
- Places to work on gear and outfits, and to store buildouts.
- Hang out with friends.
- Modify fighters, drop ships and ground vehicles, if equipped.
- A few onboard minigames: arcade-style ones, plus a citizen-science style research puzzle. (Aaron's references are in the inspiration doc's notes, not in game content.)
