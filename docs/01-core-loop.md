# 01 — Core Loop
**Minute to minute (pilot):** undock, travel, then do an activity (mine, trade, hunt bounties, scan), take risk, and come back with cargo or credits.
**Hour to hour (industrialist):** spend raw resources on colony upgrades, queue research, and run fabrication jobs that build ships and modules.
**Day to day (strategist):** send timed fleet missions (transport, colonize, raid, escort), keep up with Syndicate ops, and defend territory.

**Session to session (captain):** run origin and companion story chapters, raise crew affinity, choose station assignments, and take instanced away missions or boarding actions.

The loops feed each other. Colonies produce the raw materials that industry needs. Industry builds the ships that pilots fly and lose, and those losses create demand. Pilots escort and haul between colonies and markets. Territory decides who gets the rich systems.

Offline play: colony timers and fleet missions keep resolving on the server. Your ship docks safely whenever you log out in a station.

Crew add a layer across all loops. Officers boost your ship in combat and industry, and story choices change your reputation and market contacts.
