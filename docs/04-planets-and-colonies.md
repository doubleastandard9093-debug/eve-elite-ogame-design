# 04 — Planets & Colonies
You manage colonies from the in-game **Holo-Command** UI. You can open it in any station or at a colony, and remotely in a limited, read-mostly form.
- **Colony slots:** limited by Astro-Logistics research. Planet type (rocky, ice, gas, or lava) changes resource yields and how many build fields you get.
- **Buildings:** Ferrite Mine, Crystal Extractor, Volatile Siphon, Power Plant (fusion or solar), Storage, Shipyard, Research Lab, Defense Grid, and Orbital Dock.
- **Build queue:** one building and one shipyard job at a time. Costs and build times grow with each level. Players can unlock more parallel queues.
- **Fleet missions:** Transport, Deploy, Colonize, Raid, Spy, Recycle (salvage debris), and Escort. A mission's travel time depends on distance and its slowest ship. Players can intercept fleets in real space, which ties this layer back to flight.
- **Raids:** combat between fleets and defenses resolves automatically on the server. Wrecks leave debris fields, and raiders take part of the stored resources. New players get beginner protection.

**2026-10 update:** planets are now landable and built city-style, either directly or through a governor. Landing tiers are in doc 17; planet building, governors and gas giants are in doc 19. Those docs supersede the build-field model here where they conflict.
