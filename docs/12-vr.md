# 12 — VR (PC VR via Meta Quest Link / Air Link, OpenXR)

## Scope
- **Primary mode:** seated cockpit VR. The ship is the playspace, and the player sits in the pilot seat.
- **Platform:** PC VR through Meta Quest Link (cable) or Air Link (Wi-Fi), using the Meta OpenXR runtime. Any OpenXR runtime should work, such as SteamVR, but Quest is the reference.
- **Out of scope:** a standalone Quest build (Android), and room-scale walking in the cockpit. Ground away missions are VR-supported, but polished VR ground play comes after the MVP.

## Engine setup (UE 5.8)
- **Plugins:** OpenXR and OpenXRHandTracking (optional hand presence and menu pinch). Enhanced Input supplies the XR bindings.
- **Rendering:** instanced stereo and mobile multi-view off (PC). Forward shading is recommended for VR builds, since it's cheaper and supports MSAA, but we'll evaluate it against deferred and Lumen per scene.
- **Foveation:** fixed foveated rendering through variable rate shading where the GPU and runtime support it. Eye-tracked foveation applies only on Quest Pro, after the MVP.

## Pawn & camera
- `AHDShipPawn` has a `CockpitRoot` scene component at the pilot's eye anchor, with a `CockpitCamera` child that has **Lock to HMD** turned on. In VR the HMD pose moves the camera relative to the seat. On a flat screen the camera sits at the eye anchor.
- **Recenter:** a key or controller binding calls `RecenterVR()`, which resets HMD orientation and position to the seat. It also runs automatically on possession.
- Ship rotation is applied to the root, so the cockpit moves with the ship and the player's head stays local to it. That keeps the cockpit as a stable frame of reference.

## Input
1. **Motion controllers:** you grab the virtual stick (right) and throttle (left) with grip. Hand pose maps to pitch, roll and yaw (twist), and throttle pushes forward and back. Buttons on the virtual grips cover fire, boost and abilities. Release the grip and the stick re-centers.
2. **HOTAS:** the best experience, and needs no headset-specific bindings.
3. **Gamepad:** fully supported.

All three map to the same Enhanced Input actions through separate mapping contexts.

## UI
- **Diegetic first:** HUD elements are 3D widget components on cockpit surfaces (radar, shield-facing display, power pips, target panel). Laser-pointer or touch interaction is planned.
- **Holo-Command in VR:** a holotable that pops up in station or cockpit (a 3D widget plus a 3D system map) to manage colonies, queues and fleets. Hand pinch or laser selects.
- Text stays at a minimum angular size, and panels sit 0.8–1.5 m from the eye.

## Comfort
- Vignette that scales with angular velocity and acceleration (adjustable 0–100%).
- The cockpit frame and a horizon or galaxy-plane reference line act as anchors.
- **Ground play:** snap turn (30/45°) or smooth turn, and teleport or smooth locomotion.
- Optional seated-height calibration, and an option to reduce camera shake.

## Performance targets
- **72 fps minimum, 90 fps target** on a Quest 2 or 3 over Link, recommended spec around an RTX 3070.
- Budget about 11 ms per frame at 90 fps.
- Instanced stereo, HLOD, aggressive LODs for ships, and limited dynamic shadows in space.
- Profile with Unreal Insights and the OpenXR frame timing tools.
