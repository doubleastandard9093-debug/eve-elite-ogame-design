# 07 — Progression & Risk
- **Pilot skills** improve with use and through optional training queues. They unlock hulls and module tiers, and specializing pays off more than generalizing.
- **Risk:** when your ship is destroyed, the hull and fitted modules are lost. Part of the loot drops as a wreck, and your pod respawns at your home clone station.
- **Lost hulls stay lost, but keep residual value:** wrecks become salvage, and good hulls can be rebuilt with a "recovered" tag (doc 16).
- **Insurance:** pays back part of the hull's base value.
- **Assets** stay in the region you left them in.
- **Safety nets:** a starter hull that is free and replaceable, beginner colony protection for 7 days or until you reach a point threshold, and Secured for low-risk play.
- **Reputation** with NPC factions unlocks missions and equipment.

## Crew & companions
- Crew level up through use and missions, unlocking higher-rank abilities and trait slots.
- Companion affinity tiers unlock personal arc chapters and signature abilities.
- Crew risk is softer than ship risk. Crew can be wounded or captured (with a rescue mission) but are never permanently lost. Regular recruits can be killed in Frontier if you choose hardcore rules.

## Story & origin arcs
- Each origin arc grants a starter hull variant, one companion, and initial faction standings.
- Story progress gives reputation, blueprints, and crew, but never better gear than the sandbox offers. That keeps the economy player-driven.
- Choice flags affect the rest of the game (doc 10).
