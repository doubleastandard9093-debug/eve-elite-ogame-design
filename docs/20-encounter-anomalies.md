# 20 — Encounter Anomalies
Source: Aaron's design brief, 2026-10-28.

Encounter anomalies replace big salvage inside Secured space, and then keep going. Class 3 and 4 hulks stop at the Secured border; these don't. There are four site families, scanned down the same way as wormholes, and **each family spawns in every band**. Security changes the rules of the room. It does not delete the room.

## The four families
| Family | What it is | Play |
|---|---|---|
| **Signal fires** | A distress call, a lane beacon gone quiet, a convoy that stopped answering. | Assist, escort or tow. The day-one combat site: NPCs are already losing when you arrive. |
| **Contraband drifts** | Unmarked cargo, a cut-open hauler, a fence finishing the transfer. | Scoop it, fight it, or report it to the local authority for standing. Reporting pays less and is the Secured-legal version of the site. |
| **Rogue nests** | A squadron that has stopped pretending to be traders. | Clear the nest. Not a rebuildable hull, so it never competes with the recovered-hull faucet. |
| **Dead signatures** | Half-collapsed jump scars, ancient buoys, stellar noise that is not quite a wormhole. | Loot is components, data, and occasionally a chart toward a real wormhole or a ruin gate. |

## What changes by band
- **Secured:** all four families, PVE only. System authority is already on site, or comes when called. No player may shoot another, duel flag aside. Payout is the floor of the table. The point is to teach the scan, the approach and the fight without getting jumped.
- **Restricted:** harder NPC tables, better components. Contesting another player needs a declared war with 48 h notice; an unwarred pilot is not a legal target. Reporting a contraband drift still works and pays less than keeping it.
- **Borderland:** inside an announced claim the site is open, and the claim owner can toll it like a lane. Outside a claim it stays PVE against NPCs. Payout steps up. Nests are big enough that a wing is the right answer and a solo frigate is a scout.
- **Frontier:** uncapped NPC table, best component rolls, open. Dead signatures here are the ones that resolve into hidden wormholes. A nest in The Black may field a capital-scale NPC hull: the fight Class 4 used to promise, without a recoverable capital wreck in Secured space.

## Two rules that protect salvage
1. An encounter anomaly **never drops a rebuildable hull**. A towable capital does not exist in Secured space at all.
2. The anomaly **scan signature is distinct from a hulk signature**, so a pilot knows which profession they're about to fly before warping in.
